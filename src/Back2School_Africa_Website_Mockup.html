Our purpose

Education can restore dignity—and create possibility.

Back2SchoolAfrica is an education-focused non-profit working to help people learn, build skills and access opportunities. The website will bring the organisation’s mission, programmes and community stories together in one clear place.

One organisation. Many ways to move forward.

From a child joining a reading club to a young person preparing a university application, each programme can have a clear page explaining who it supports, how it works and how the community can help.
